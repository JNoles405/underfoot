# Underfoot

An ant farm between two panes of glass, in the browser.

The colony digs tunnels on its own. Drop crumbs on the surface and the workers carry
them down to the queen. Every two crumbs become an egg, and every egg hatches into
another digger. You can also pour sand, water and stone, add ants, or erase.

- Keys 1–6 pick a material, T toggles pheromone trails, N toggles day and night, Space pauses.
- Ants carrying a crumb home leave a violet scent trail that other workers follow back to the food.
- A day lasts four minutes: the sun and moon cross the sky, stars come out, and the colony rests after dark.
- The speed button goes 1×, 2×, 4× and Real speed, where ants walk about 2 cm a second like a garden ant (the farm is drawn at 2 mm a cell, about 40 × 26 cm).
- Packed sand holds its shape; poured sand is loose until it lands.
- Water soaks into the sand. Ants left under water too long drown.
- Workers live a few minutes, so the colony needs feeding.
